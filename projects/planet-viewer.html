Procedural Planet Viewer

A real-time procedural planet with an atmosphere, oceans, clouds, an orbiting moon, and a flyable spacecraft.

OVERVIEW

The planet is generated entirely on the GPU. An icosphere mesh is deformed in the vertex shader with layered noise to form terrain, then shaded with elevation-based colors and triplanar texturing. Around it sit an ocean layer, clouds, and atmospheric scattering. A moon orbits as a scene graph node, and the player can fly a spacecraft between the two bodies.

TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS

  • Vertex-shader terrain displacement using noise on an icosphere
  • Procedural elevation-based coloring with triplanar texture mapping
  • Atmospheric scattering, ocean, and cloud layers
  • Orbiting moon built as a scene graph node
  • Flyable spacecraft with sphere-of-influence gravity per body and bounding-sphere collision

CHALLENGES & SOLUTIONS

I first tried GPU transform feedback so collisions would follow the shader-displaced terrain, but it proved unstable. I replaced it with bounding-sphere collision, which was simpler and reliable, and let me spend the time on the visuals and flight model.
