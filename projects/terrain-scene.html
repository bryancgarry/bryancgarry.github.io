TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS

  • View frustum culling built from camera geometry vectors
  • LOD sphere nodes selected by distance thresholds
  • Diamond-Square fractal terrain with trees placed by elevation band
  • Catmull-Rom spline camera flythrough with a look-ahead fix across segments

CHALLENGES & SOLUTIONS

The standard approach of extracting frustum planes from the projection-view matrix (Gribb-Hartmann) failed because the framework used a non-standard column-major accessor. I rebuilt the planes directly from the camera's position and basis vectors instead.
