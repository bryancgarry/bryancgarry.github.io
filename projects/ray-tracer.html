TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS

  • Blinn-Phong and Cook-Torrance BRDF shading
  • Shadow rays, recursive reflection and refraction
  • Triangle mesh intersection with Möller–Trumbore, accelerated by AABB bounding volumes
  • Quadric ellipsoid surfaces and procedural checkerboard textures

CHALLENGES & SOLUTIONS

Debugging included fixing reversed refraction entry/exit logic, a negative-modulo bug in the checkerboard texture, and salt-and-pepper artifacts caused by incorrect scaling in the ray-triangle intersection math.
